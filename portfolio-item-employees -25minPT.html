Exercise 4 Part 3 Summary

Locations to Reach Grocery Store

The study uses the pedestrian network datasets and closest facility network analysis to find locations to reach a grocery store in 7-10 minutes walking. Grocery_Food is imported to facilities, and GridpointTrondheim_250 is imported to incidents. The closest facility analysis is run in a direction towards the facilities by setting an 8-minute cutoff. The result of total time and total length was calculated and added to the route feature under closest facility. The record in the incident was joined with the route record. 669 grid points have access to the grocery stores in less than 8 minutes by foot.

Locations to Reach Grocery Store by Bike

The study uses the bike network datasets and closest facility network analysis to delineate the population grid points that can access grocery stores within 4-7 minutes by bike. Grocery_Food is imported to facilities, and GridpointTrondheim_250 is imported to incidents. The closest facility analysis is run in a direction towards the facilities by setting a 5-minute cutoff. The result of total time and total length was calculated and added to the route feature under closest facility. The record in the incident was joined with the route record. 1458 grid points have access to the grocery stores within 5 minutes by bike.

Points with Non-Food Stores Reachable by Car

The car network datasets and OD cost matrix analysis were used to find points with non-food stores reachable within 7-11 minutes by car. GridpointTrondheim_250 was imported to the origin point, and Retail_non_food is imported into destinations in the OD-cost matrix. The OD-cost matrix analysis is run by setting a 10-minute cutoff. The result of total time and total length was calculated and added to the line feature. The calculated total time was adjusted by adding 4 minutes for parking and walking. The line feature within 10 minutes was selected and summarized. 2381 grid points have access to non-food stores within 10 minutes by car.

Employees Reachable by Public Transit

The public transit network, GridpointTrondheim_250, Employees_100grid, and OD-cost matrix analysis were used to delineate the population grid points that can reach employees within 25 minutes by public transit during morning rush hour. GridpointTrondheim_250 was imported to the origin point, and Employees_100 grid is imported into destination in the OD-cost matrix. The OD-cost matrix analysis is run using the OD-cost matrix on March 29, 2023, at 7:30 AM. The results were calculated and added to the line feature under the OD-cost matrix. The line feature data is joined with destination. 3810 grid points have access to employees within 25 minutes. A maximum of 104564 employees can be reached within the specified period.
